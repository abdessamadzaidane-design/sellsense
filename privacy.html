Privacy Policy

English

SellSense ("we", "us") helps you understand your real net profit. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, display name, business type, currency preference, language.
  • Business data you enter: project names, revenue and cost figures, saved periods/calculations.
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details. We store your subscription status only.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP, user agent) used for security and abuse prevention.

2. How we use data

  • To operate the service: store your projects and calculations, enforce plan limits, process sign-in.
  • To process payments via Stripe (subscription status, invoices).
  • To keep the service secure (rate limiting, abuse detection).
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use your business figures for advertising.

3. Storage & security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access to your data is protected by per-user row-level security.

4. Your rights

  • Access & export: use "Export my data" in the dashboard to download everything we hold about you.
  • Deletion: use "Delete my account" in the dashboard to permanently erase your account and all associated data.
  • Correction: update your profile details in the dashboard at any time.
  • If you are in the EU/EEA, you may also contact us to exercise any GDPR right.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in and remember your language. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy.

6. Contact

Email: support@sellsenseapp.com — we respond within 24 hours.
