Quick answers.

FAQs from founders like you.

Is SellSense really free?
Yes — fully free, no credit card, no trial countdowns. All features are unlocked: unlimited products, AI Decision Center, all reports, 27 currencies, team seats, integrations. We may introduce optional add-ons later, but the core platform stays free.
How does the AI Decision Center decide what to recommend?
It looks at per-product margin, trend, return rate, CPA, and historical pattern, then ranks opportunities by projected dollar impact. Every recommendation comes with a confidence score and the exact data behind it. You can ignore, snooze, or apply with one click.
Do you support COD (Cash-on-Delivery)?
Yes — SellSense is one of the only tools built with COD-first logic: failed delivery rates, packaging costs per order, carrier-specific shipping, and CMI fees are all native cost categories. Designed with Morocco, Egypt, Algeria and other COD markets in mind.
Can I switch currencies after I've added data?
Absolutely. Click the currency picker in the top bar — every number on the page re-renders in your chosen currency using live FX rates. Your underlying data stays in your primary currency.
How is my data secured?
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We never sell or share your data. You can export everything any time or delete your account with one click — no questions asked.
Can I import data from Shopify, Excel or another tool?
Yes. Connect Shopify directly for auto-sync, or import a CSV/Excel file from Settings → Data. We have templates for the most common formats. WooCommerce, YouCan, and Stripe integrations are also available.
Does SellSense work for content creators and freelancers?
Yes. Each business type gets tailored input fields: creators track AdSense, sponsors, courses & merch; freelancers track effective hourly rate, Upwork/Fiverr fees, FX losses & subcontractors. Or run multiple workspaces — one per business.
What languages and regions do you support?
8 languages (English, French, Arabic with RTL, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Hindi, Chinese) and 27 currencies covering most of the world. Date, number & currency formatting is localized.
