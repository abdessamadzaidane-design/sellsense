Quick answers.

FAQs from founders like you.

Is SellSense really free?
Yes — fully free, no credit card, no trial countdowns. All features are unlocked: unlimited products, AI Decision Center, all reports, 27 currencies, team seats, integrations.
How does the AI Decision Center decide what to recommend?
It looks at per-product margin, trend, return rate, CPA, and historical pattern, then ranks opportunities by projected dollar impact. Every recommendation comes with a confidence score and the exact data behind it.
Do you support COD (Cash-on-Delivery)?
Yes — SellSense is one of the only tools built with COD-first logic: failed delivery rates, packaging costs per order, carrier-specific shipping, and CMI fees are all native cost categories.
Can I switch currencies after I've added data?
Click the currency picker in the top bar — every number on the page re-renders in your chosen currency using live FX rates.
How is my data secured?
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). You can export everything anytime or delete your account with one click.
Can I import data from Shopify, Excel or another tool?
Yes. Connect Shopify directly for auto-sync, or import a CSV/Excel file from Settings → Data. WooCommerce, YouCan, and Stripe integrations are also available.
Does SellSense work for content creators and freelancers?
Yes. Each business type gets tailored input fields. Creators track AdSense, sponsors, courses & merch. Freelancers track effective hourly rate, Upwork/Fiverr fees, FX losses.
What languages and regions do you support?
8 languages (English, French, Arabic with RTL, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Hindi, Chinese) and 27 currencies covering most of the world.
