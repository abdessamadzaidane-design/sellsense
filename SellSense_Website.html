The problem

Founders are drowning in tools, blind to causes.

You run a real business across Shopify, Stripe, Meta, Google, and ten other tools. Each one shows you a different number. None of them tells you why — let alone what to do next.

PROBLEM 01

The Data Tax

Eight to fourteen tools, each with its own version of revenue. You reconcile in spreadsheets every week and never fully trust the result.

PROBLEM 02

The Causation Gap

Profit just dropped 18%. Was it traffic? Conversion? A specific SKU? You guess. Most of the time, you guess wrong.

PROBLEM 03

The Decision Bottleneck

Should you scale this ad? Pause that SKU? Without a finance team to model the tradeoffs, you freeze — or move blind.

PROBLEM 04

The Lag

By the time you notice that a creative burned out or a cohort started churning, you've lost two weeks and thousands of dollars.

PROBLEM 05

The Reporting Burden

Weekly reports for your co-founder. Monthly reports for your investors. You build them by hand. They're never quite right.

THE FIX

One AI Brain

SellSense ingests every source, normalizes to a canonical truth, and produces explained, citation-backed decisions — in your language, every morning.

Relationships, not isolated metrics.

SellSense models every KPI as a node in a directed graph. When revenue moves, the engine walks the graph automatically — sessions → conversion → AOV — until it lands on a single, actionable root cause.

When churn ticks up, the engine quantifies how that compounds backward into LTV, and forward into your runway. You see the leverage before it costs you.
