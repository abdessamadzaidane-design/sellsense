English

1. Cookies

SellSense does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use only what is strictly necessary to run the service:

  • Authentication (local storage): keeps you signed in. No cookie is set for this; we use the browser's local storage.
  • Language preference (local storage): remembers whether you chose English or French.

2. Third-party services

  • Supabase (hosting/auth/database) — may set technical cookies/local storage for session handling.
  • Stripe — only on payment pages; Stripe may set its own cookies there.
  • Google Fonts — font delivery; Google may log requests per its own privacy policy.

3. Managing storage

You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings at any time. Signing out also clears your session. Clearing storage does not delete your account data on our servers.

4. Contact

support@sellsenseapp.com
